# Listed Company Financial Statement Analysis: Infosys Limited

**Reporting period:** FY2025-26 compared with FY2024-25, year ended March 31  
**Basis:** Consolidated Ind AS statements unless noted. Currency values are ₹ crore except EPS.  
**Purpose:** Educational financial-statement interpretation, not investment advice. No recommendation is made to buy, sell or hold shares.

## Company and source notes

Infosys Limited (NSE/BSE symbol: INFY) is a listed technology and consulting services company. The figures below are taken from its published consolidated audited Ind AS results for the year ended March 31, 2026 and its official annual-report snapshot. Primary sources: [FY2025-26 Ind AS financial results](https://www.infosys.com/investors/reports-filings/quarterly-results/2025-2026/q4/ind-as.html), [FY2025-26 IFRS financial snapshot](https://www.infosys.com/about/last-fiscal.html), and [Integrated Annual Report 2025-26](https://www.infosys.com/investors/reports-filings/annual-report/annual-reports/ar-2025-26.html).

## 1. Profit and Loss statement

| Metric | FY2024-25 | FY2025-26 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue from operations | 162,990 | 178,650 | 9.6% |
| Total expenses | 128,982 | 141,688 | 9.9% |
| Profit before exceptional item and tax | 37,608 | 41,284 | 9.8% |
| Profit for the year | 26,750 | 29,474 | 10.2% |
| Profit attributable to owners | 26,713 | 29,440 | 10.2% |
| Basic EPS | ₹64.50 | ₹71.58 | 11.0% |

**Interpretation.** Revenue increased 9.6%, while profit for the year rose 10.2%; earnings grew a little faster than revenue. The profit-for-year margin was approximately 16.41% in FY2024-25 and 16.50% in FY2025-26, a modest improvement. Total expenses grew around 9.9%, broadly close to the revenue growth rate. These figures describe historical performance, not future results.

## 2. Balance Sheet

| Metric | FY2024-25 | FY2025-26 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Total assets | 148,903 | 155,967 | 4.7% |
| Total current assets | 97,099 | 103,489 | 6.6% |
| Total equity (including non-controlling interest) | 96,203 | 93,297 | -3.0% |
| Current liabilities | 42,850 | 52,322 | 22.1% |
| Cash and cash equivalents | 24,455 | 22,201 | -9.2% |
| Trade receivables | 31,158 | 35,234 | 13.1% |

**Liquidity proxy.** Current ratio = current assets ÷ current liabilities. It moved from 2.27x to 1.98x. It remains above 1.0x, but the decline suggests current liabilities increased faster than current assets. The ratio is only a broad liquidity indicator; asset quality, receivable ageing, investment liquidity and the timing of obligations matter too.

Trade receivables increased faster than revenue. This is worth monitoring through DSO (days sales outstanding), ageing buckets, contract terms and collection rates. That does not prove collection problems, but it is a sensible follow-up analysis.

## 3. Cash Flow Statement

| Metric | FY2024-25 | FY2025-26 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Net cash generated by operating activities | 35,694 | 33,986 | -4.8% |
| Capital expenditure (cash outflow magnitude) | 2,237 | 2,727 | 21.9% |
| Simple FCF = operating cash flow - capex | 33,457 | 31,259 | -6.6% |
| Net cash from investing activities | (1,946) | 1,946 | Direction changed |
| Net cash used in financing activities | (24,161) | (39,786) | Greater outflow |
| Cash and cash equivalents at year end | 24,455 | 22,201 | -9.2% |

Operating cash flow decreased 4.8% even as accounting profit increased. The company's cash flow statement shows a larger negative working-capital movement in trade receivables and unbilled revenue in FY2025-26. Financing outflows were larger and included dividend payments and a share buyback. These are cash-allocation choices; an analyst should reconcile them to the detailed statements and notes rather than viewing every outflow as negative by itself.

The simple FCF above is calculated from the same Ind AS cash flow statement as operating cash flow less property/equipment/intangible capex. The company's separate FY2026 integrated-report headline reports free cash flow of ₹33,097 crore under its stated reporting definition. Do not mix headline metrics across accounting/reporting bases without reconciliation.

## 5. Conclusions and next questions

1. **Growth:** Revenue, profit and EPS rose year on year. Compare constant-currency growth and segment/client trends before explaining drivers.
2. **Margin:** Profit margin was broadly stable to slightly higher on this simple calculation. Review employee costs, subcontractor spend, pricing and utilisation to understand operating leverage.
3. **Liquidity / working capital:** Current ratio declined; cash equivalents fell and receivables rose. Examine ageing and collection performance, not only headline balances.
4. **Cash conversion:** Operating cash flow fell despite higher profit. Reconcile net income to cash from operations and quantify working-capital drivers.
5. **Capital allocation:** The company reported dividend and buyback activity. Model effects separately from operating performance.
6. **Limitations:** Two annual observations are not enough for a forecast. Accounting policy, one-off charges, foreign exchange, acquisitions and group-vs-standalone differences affect comparisons.
